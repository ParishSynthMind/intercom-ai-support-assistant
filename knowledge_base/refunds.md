# Refunds and billing

## Refund window
You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment on any plan. After 14 days we do not refund the current month, but you can cancel so that you are not charged again.

## Charged twice
If you see two charges for the same month, one is usually a card pre-authorisation that drops off within 3 to 5 working days. If both are still there after 5 working days, reply with the last 4 digits of the card and the dates, and the billing team will refund the duplicate.

## Invoices
Invoices are in Settings > Billing > Invoices. You can add a VAT number and company address there; new invoices show them, old ones are not re-issued.
