# Plans and cancelling

## Cancel your subscription
Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and you will not be charged again.

## Upgrade or downgrade
Upgrades take effect at once and you pay the difference for the rest of the month. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

## Exporting your data
Before cancelling you can download everything in Settings > Data > Export. The export is a ZIP of CSV files and is ready within an hour.
