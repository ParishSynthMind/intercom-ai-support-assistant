# Account and sign-in

## Reset your password
Go to the sign-in page and click "Forgot password". We email a reset link that works for 30 minutes. If it does not arrive, check spam and make sure you are using the email address on the account.

## Two-factor authentication
If you lost your phone, use one of the backup codes you saved when you turned on two-factor authentication. Without a backup code, the account owner must contact support from the account email address so we can verify it.

## Change your email address
Go to Settings > Profile > Email. We send a confirmation link to the new address; the change only happens once you click it.
