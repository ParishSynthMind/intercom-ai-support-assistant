## Upwork (portfolio project)
**Title:** Intercom AI support assistant: Claude triage, cited draft replies, human approval

**Role:** AI integration developer

**Description (under 600 characters):**
An AI layer for a support team on Intercom. Signed webhooks bring each new conversation in; Claude classifies intent, priority and sentiment, tags it (team routing optional), finds the answer in the help articles and writes a draft reply with its sources as an internal note. Nothing reaches the customer until a person approves it, and it is sent once. Opt-out list, prompt-injection guard, AI disclosure, kill switch, no personal data in logs. Tested live on a real Intercom workspace with real Claude; 198 automated tests.
